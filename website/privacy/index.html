Privacy Policy — DocForge

Last updated: March 2026
Developer: ZAYA CODE HUB
Contact: support@pdftoolbox.app

Overview

DocForge — PDF Editor & Tools processes your PDFs, images, and scans on your device. We do not upload your files to our servers for conversion, compression, or editing.

Data processed on your device

  • PDF and image tools (merge, split, compress, convert, scan, OCR, batch)
  • Recent file history (filename, path, size — stored locally)
  • App preferences and optional vault entries
  • Cached enhancement and temporary processing files

You can clear history and cache from Settings.

Permissions

  • Camera — only when you start document scanning
  • Files — via Android system picker (no broad storage access required)
  • Biometric — optional app lock using device secure hardware

Advertising (free version)

The free app may show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data per Google's Privacy Policy. Consent is requested where required via Google's User Messaging Platform. Pro removes ads.

In-app purchases

Pro upgrades use Google Play Billing. Google processes payments. We receive purchase tokens to verify Pro access on your device. We do not store payment card details.

Third-party services

  • Google Mobile Ads (advertising)
  • Google Play Billing (purchases)
  • Google ML Kit (on-device text recognition)
  • Syncfusion PDF components (on-device PDF processing)

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
